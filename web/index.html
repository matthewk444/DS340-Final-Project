      How to play

      1. 1
        Get a word. Each round gives you something simple to draw — a cat, a house, a mailbox…
      2. 2
        Doodle it on the whiteboard. You have 20 seconds, starting from your first stroke. Draw it the way you normally would!
      3. 3
        Watch Pip guess live. The AI names your doodle while you draw, and the world map glows where it thinks you're from.
      4. 4
        Six doodles, one verdict. After the last round, Pip makes a final guess with a confidence score — then you tell it if it was right.

      Guessing a region from a doodle is hard — small habits (which way you draw a circle, what your mailbox looks like) are the only clues. Treat it as a fun experiment, not a passport check.
